01 — The Kura鯉山酒造

A small brewhouse above a spring-fed pond, where the water reaches the koi before it reaches the rice.

Four brewers, one winter, no shortcuts. We steam Omachi rice at dawn, grow koji by hand and let each tank find its own pace.

The kura has brewed on this hillside since 1911. The pond came first: spring water at nine degrees, soft enough that the koi have outlived three head brewers.
