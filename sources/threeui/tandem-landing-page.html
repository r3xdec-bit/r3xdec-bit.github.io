      01 — Philosophy

      Small on purpose.

      We build for a handful of people at a time. A release has to be loved by five before it is seen by five thousand, so every product starts as a room, not a platform.

      02 — Approach

      Prototype. Ship. Listen. Repeat.

      A working build on Tuesday beats a deck on Friday. We ship weekly, watch people use it in the room with us, and cut what nobody touches.
