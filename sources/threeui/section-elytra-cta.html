08 — FAQ

ASKED&/ANSWERED

Still curious? A human answers within the hour, day or night.

Does Elytra replace my antivirus?

It can. The agent includes next-generation prevention, but it also runs politely alongside the tools you already have while you migrate.

How long does deployment take?

About four minutes per fleet policy. Most teams are fully covered before the end of day one.

Where does my data live?

In the region you choose. Imperial plans can keep the emerald core entirely on your own hardware.

What happens during an attack?

Glass-wing playbooks isolate the device, revoke risky sessions and page a human, all in a 38 ms median.

Can I try it without a card?

Yes. Larva is free forever for up to five endpoints, no card and no sales call.

Do you help with compliance audits?

Evidence for common frameworks is collected continuously and exported in one click, so audit week stops being a week.
