Passage I

The source · Before sunrise

The river wakes before the mountain.

At first light, five fish turn against the current. One carries a red crown—a mark old enough to be mistaken for memory.

Passage II

The temple · Silver rain

The temple waits beneath the mist.

Stone lanterns count each ripple. The gate opens only for what arrives without a sound, carrying nothing but the river.

Passage III

The grove · Late light

The bamboo gate remembers every name.

Beyond the pond, the water narrows. Bamboo closes overhead; the red mark becomes the final light and the path becomes home.

Epilogue · The remembered current

Every river is a way home.

The koi passes beneath the final gate. The water closes without a seam, but somewhere downstream a red moon trembles again.
