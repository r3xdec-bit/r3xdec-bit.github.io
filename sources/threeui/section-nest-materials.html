Architecture / 001

More Than a House.
A World Within.

A living architectural experience designed around space, light, nature and the moments between them.

01 / Architecture

Designed from
the outside in.

A cantilevered volume of cedar and concrete, anchored by a hand-laid stone hearth and crowned with a garden and a warm stone spa. Every room faces the valley; none faces another.

12.0 × 9.0 m
Two storeys + roof
Cantilever 1.5 m

Three gestures

  1. i

    Anchor

    A dry-laid stone hearth roots the house to the rock it stands on.

  2. ii

    Lift

    The upper floor reaches past its base, out toward the view.

  3. iii

    Crown

    A garden on the roof returns the ground the house occupies.

See everything.
Hide nothing.

Hover the layers. Every part is drawn, named and placed.

03 / Material

Built to be
touched.

Five materials, chosen to age rather than wear. Nothing is painted; everything is what it appears to be.

Material schedule

  1. iEuropean oakOiled · quarter sawn
  2. iiLocal stoneHand-split · dry laid
  3. iiiConcreteBoard-formed · in situ
  4. ivGlassLow-iron · triple glazed
  5. vLinenBelgian flax · stonewashed

Philosophy

Light on the land.
Built to outlast us.

  • Sun

    Cedar louvres shade the summer sun and let the low winter sun reach the floor.

  • Air

    Warm air rises through the stair to the skylight; the house cools itself.

  • Matter

    Stone from the site, timber from managed forests, no finish that needs repainting.

  • Ground

    The roof garden replaces the meadow the house was built on.
