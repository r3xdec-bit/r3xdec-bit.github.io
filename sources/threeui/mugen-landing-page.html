MUGEN

An immersive five-scene journey through gate, path, garden, hall, and eternity, staging a fully procedural Japanese world behind flat monumental type, vertical chapter markers, a compact menu, and an authored loading sequence. The senbon-torii walk is paved in procedural flags that follow the corridor's own sway and rise, and the hero gate carries its roof as one swept tapered sweep.

Runtime Full HTML + DOM/CSS + Three.js r160
Interaction Original menu and chapter navigation hovers, eased scroll progression, restrained pointer camera response, and responsive layout
