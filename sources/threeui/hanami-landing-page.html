Hanami  花見

Shorter than a week

The window between first bloom and petal fall is seven days. We plan inside it, not around it.

Held, not booked

Your week stays open until the front is called. Then we place you where the petals actually are — no change fees, no calendar guesswork.

Quiet by design

Same trees, different hour. We route you to the mornings nobody photographs.

Just look up

Nothing to memorise. A map, a time, and a bench that faces the right way.

Petals, then water

When the fall begins the river takes them. Hanaikada — the flower raft — lasts a single afternoon, and we plan for that afternoon too.

One tree at a time

Fifty-eight stations, Kagoshima to Matsumae. The front climbs a hundred miles a week and we climb with it.

Nothing to catch

Missed mornings replanned overnight. If the front moves, so do you.

*We hold your week unconfirmed until the front is called, then move you to the morning that blooms. Kyoto in 2025 opened four days early; everyone we held arrived on the third day of full bloom.

Lighter on the Places That Let Us In

The famous trees are photographed a thousand times an hour.

We send you to the other ones — the temple courtyard two stops earlier, the riverbank that empties at nine, the avenue a town planted for itself. Same season, fewer shoulders, and a place that is glad you came.
