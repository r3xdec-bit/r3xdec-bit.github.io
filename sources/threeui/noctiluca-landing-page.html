Every light
down here
is alive

Past two hundred metres the sun stops being a source. Everything below that line is chemistry — one cell deciding to burn, and the whole column answering it.

The deck — 06 specimens

Six ways
to make light

None of these animals is lit. Every colour you can see here is being manufactured, on purpose, by the thing you are looking at.

011 000 m

The alarm

Atolla wyvillei

Grabbed, it fires a rotating blue wheel — not to get away, but to call in something bigger than whatever has hold of it.

02800 m

The prism

Bathocyroe fosteri

It makes no colour at all. Eight rows of beating combs split whatever light reaches them; the rainbow is a side effect of swimming.

03400 m

The countershade

Watasenia scintillans

It lights its own underside to match the surface above, so nothing hunting from below is ever given a silhouette to aim at.

042 000 m

The lure

Melanocetus johnsonii

The light on the rod is not hers. It is a colony of bacteria she keeps — housed and fed — and the glow is the rent.

05700 m

The chain

Praya dubia

Forty metres of linked clones, each one a specialist. The pulse travels the whole length like a message being handed along.

06Surface

The bloom

Noctiluca scintillans

Sea sparkle. Disturb the water at night and the whole surface answers — the only one on this list you can meet from a beach.
