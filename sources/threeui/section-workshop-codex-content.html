Sheet 05Manifest

I founded DesignCode after traveling for two years across the globe, teaching designers code and developers design.

In the era of AI, better prompting is only the start. The real edge is persistence, sharper workflows, and staying current while the tools change under your hands.

01

Designers need code intuition. Developers need design taste. Builders need both.

02

Prompting is a craft. Persistence is the multiplier. Workflow is how the work compounds.

03

The new studio is not a room. It is a stack of agents, references, templates, habits, and shipped products.
