What Kerrline is

Your GPUs talk over copper. Ours talk in light.

Kerrline is the network inside an AI cluster. An optical engine beside each GPU turns its data into 64 colours of laser light. All 64 share one strand of glass the width of a hair, run up to 2 km, and split apart again at the next GPU. No copper traces, no retimers, almost no heat.
