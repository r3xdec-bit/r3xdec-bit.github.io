Teams that stopped keeping three copies

Lumen Freight, Corvid, Fernhill, Fieldwork, Mosaic Health, Oriel, Quarry, Tallyhouse, Brightwater

Most teams keep three copies of the truth: the doc where it was decided, the board where it’s tracked, and the thread where it quietly changed. Plumb keeps one, and it’s the one you wrote.

How it works

From a sentence to a schedule, without leaving the page.

Write Mark Plan

  1. 1.0

    Write it the way it was said.

    Start with the meeting, not the ticket. Half sentences, open questions, the thing the customer said about dates. Plumb doesn’t ask you to sort it yet.

  2. 2.0

    Mark the lines that are work.

    Select a line and press P. It becomes a task, keeps its sentence, and picks up an owner from whoever you mention.

  3. 3.0

    They land on one plan.

    Every marked line from every notebook lands on the same timeline. Drag it to another week and the note updates to match.

Write the way you think. Your notes stay in your hand, and every word is searchable the moment it’s on the page, crossings-out and all.
Circle a line to make it a task. Draw a loop around anything and it becomes a task. The name you wrote beside it becomes the owner.
