The design system, read as a feed
Chirp Ledger
@chirpledger · Light-mode, text-forward

White carries the page. Color is a signal, not decoration. Here is how the whole system holds together in a single reading column — no banner, no wash, just ink and weight.

1. Near-white surfaces run about 88% of the rendered field. Everything else earns its place against that calm.

2. One condensed sans carries all reading text at a nearly uniform fifteen pixels. Hierarchy comes from weight and spacing, never scale jumps.

3. Sky blue is rationed to links and hover states — a few words at a time, never a fill.

Hairline rules separate every timeline unit.
Soft-elevated white panels float structured data above the feed.
A single black capsule is the one high-contrast object on the page.

Surface & depth

Large radii, barely-there lift

Elevation is restrained to one soft, wide, low-opacity shadow. Radii carry the character — generous on panels, zero on media.

Motion

Sub-200ms, one curve

Utilitarian and fast. A short ease-out recolors on hover; transforms run a touch slower on press. No springs, no overshoot — motion signals state, not delight.
