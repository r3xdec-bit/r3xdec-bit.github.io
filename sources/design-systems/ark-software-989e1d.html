ARK Software

Dark-default near-monochrome product system for ARK tools, HQ and client portals: the IBM Plex superfamily in three widths, monospace chrome that labels rather than speaks, achromatic neutrals and a near-invisible derived hairline, zero elevation, cuts instead of transitions, and exactly two colours — ARK sage on the primary control and clay on failure. Values below are the dark edition; a light edition toggles via data-edition and is documented in full in the body.
