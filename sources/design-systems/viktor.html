×1

A left accordion of hairline-divided white rows paired with a right violet CTA card, across an asymmetric 80px gap. Expanded rows tint their heading violet and rotate the chevron up.

Does Viktor send without me?
Never by default — Viktor drafts and stages everything for your one-tap approval. You can enable auto-send per channel when you're ready.
Which tools does it connect to?
Gmail, Slack, Teams, your calendar and most major CRMs, connected over OAuth in a couple of clicks.
How does it learn my tone?
From your past threads, privately — the model stays scoped to your workspace and never trains a shared model.
