  • Ink on cream, held to two corner extremes

A cream-paper, ink-black editorial system. Typographic contrast carries the identity: a heavy grotesque for structure, a classic serif for voice. Color stays almost entirely achromatic, reserving warmth for a single deliberate moment.

Corners held to two extremes. Color kept in reserve.

The page's one deliberate contrast move.

Color

Ink on paper, warmth in reserve

Almost entirely achromatic — cream and near-black do the structural work. The clay hue lives in the token set as a rationed brand accent, never a rendered surface.
