A community notice board, digitised

The plain-spoken way a neighbourhood gets things done.

Newtown is where local services, programs, and notices live — organised like a paper ledger, legible as a hand-lettered sign.

Cream paper. Ink type. One red pen.

No glossy decks, no gradients for their own sake. Just the information a resident needs, set the way a printed service sheet would set it.

Photographed on Maple Street, spring session.

The vocabulary

Cream, ink, and one red.

The entire colour system: warm neutral surfaces, near-black ink, and a single rationed vermilion that carries all emphasis.

What a good notice includes

Keep it plain. A resident reading fast should know the what, the when, and the where before they finish the first line.

Everything below is optional, but every entry that has them gets read more.

  • A clear, single-sentence headline
  • An exact date and start time
  • A named place, not just a neighbourhood
  • One way to get in touch
