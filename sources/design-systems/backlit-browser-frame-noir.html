Screens inside a screen — a portfolio built as light

A near-black canvas where every project lives behind the same window. Scroll to step through the reel.

01 — Colour

A palette of light, not hue

Near-black carries every pixel. One near-white does the work of text, glow, and chrome. Saturation is rationed to a single link-blue and stays out of the shell entirely.

Surfaces

Dark fields hold the weight; elevation comes from shadow and light, not lighter greys.
