Redtigercam

REDTIGER's design system embodies bold confidence and technological trust, anchored by a striking red accent against a sophisticated dark–to–light palette. The visual identity projects premium protection and innovation—befitting a dash cam brand—through high-contrast typography, sharp geometric components, and deliberate use of negative space. The system balances aggressive energy (via #FF0000) with approachable clarity, using a pure black (#000000) as the structural backbone and white (#FFFFFF) for breathing room. Color-blocking rather than layered shadows creates depth, reinforcing a modern, no-nonsense aesthetic suited to automotive safety and reliability.
