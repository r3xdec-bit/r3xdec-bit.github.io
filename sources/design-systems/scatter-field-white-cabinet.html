A near-blank white field. A loose constellation of small objects. Whitespace as the medium, tonal contrast as the only hierarchy.

Palette

There is no hue in the chrome. Colour is rationed entirely to the content inside tiles; the interface is white, grey, and ink.

Components

The full inventory. Flat, square-edged surfaces; no borders on tiles, no shadows, no rounded corners above 4px. Chrome stays uncoloured.

Navbar

×1

Edge-to-edge, sharp-cornered, transparent sticky bar. Wordmark left, tab control beside it, status dot far right. The only full-width element in the system.

Object tile — light

×12

Flat #EDEDED mount, 0px radius, no border, no shadow. A single centered illustration fills most of the tile with an even surface margin. No caption, no CTA — a pure visual artifact.

--card-object-tile · background surface #EDEDED · radius 0 · padding 0 · no border · no shadow · type label-md

Object tile — dark

×5

Flat #000000 mount, 0px radius, no border. A near-full-bleed high-contrast image or white linework icon. Provides the only strong tonal contrast in the composition.

--card-object-tile-dark · background #000000 · radius 0 · padding 0 · no border · text on-colour #FFFFFF · type label-md

Tab control

×1

Rounded-rectangle container (radius 4px, not full pill), #EDEDED fill. The active tab merges into the background fill; inactive tabs sit as plain text with no fill. Click to switch.

--button-nav-tab · fill #EDEDED · text #000000 · radius r-control 4 · height 27 · type label-md
--button-nav-tab-inactive · fill transparent · text #000000 · radius 4 · height 27 · motion structural 0.45s var(--ease-structural)

Status dot

×1

A small solid circle, #000000 fill, full pill radius, ~6px. A minimal presence indicator with no label.

--avatar-dot · background #000000 · radius r-pill 9999 · height 6 · no label

Surface & depth

A shadowless, borderless system. Elevation is expressed only through flat tonal contrast — light tile, dark tile, white void. Radii top out at 4px.

Motion

Two curves govern the system: a slower structural curve with gentle overshoot for repositioning, and a fast standard fade for incidental state changes. State-triggered, never ambient.

Structural

transform · 0.45s · cubic-bezier(0.65, 0, 0.35, 1)

Fade

opacity · 0.3s · cubic-bezier(0.4, 0, 0.2, 1)
In composition

The whole system in one screen: a sharp edge-to-edge navbar, a hand-placed scatter cluster in the upper-middle third, and two-thirds of empty white below.
