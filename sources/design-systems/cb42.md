# cb42

> Category: Brands

> Surface: web

*Clear structure for factual, accessible communication*

A restrained Swiss-influenced web system built around a typographic grid, asymmetric composition, documentary evidence and predictable interaction.

## Color Palette

| Role             | Name       | Hex       | Usage                                                        |
| ---------------- | ---------- | --------- | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| background       | Near-white | `#fbfbfb` | Main page canvas, input fill and inverse text on red or dark fills |

## Typography

- **Display:** Akzidenz-Grotesk — weights 400, 700 — fallbacks: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- **Body:** Akzidenz-Grotesk — weights 400, 700 — fallbacks: Helvetica, Arial, sans-serif
- **Mono:** ui-monospace — weights 400, 700 — fallbacks: SFMono-Regular, Consolas, Liberation Mono, Menlo, monospace

## Voice & Tone

- **Adjectives:** objective, neutral, authoritative, functional, accessible
- **Tone:** Direct and evidence-led. State the useful fact first, distinguish observed facts from proposals, and make every action explicit.

### Messaging pillars

- Clear hierarchy and precise information establish confidence.
- Copy helps people understand, decide and act.
- Claims retain evidence, qualifications and practical consequences.
- Problems are explained without blame and paired with a useful next step.

### Vocabulary

- **Use:** clear, review, select, save, download, available after approval
- **Avoid:** best-in-class, game-changing, magical, unlock, start your journey, artificial urgency

## Imagery

- **Style:** Objective documentary photography that clearly shows relevant people, objects, places or processes.
- **Subjects:** real work and processes, relevant people in context, objects and places that support a factual claim
- **Treatment:** Preserve context and provenance, caption factual claims, retain natural color and use informative alt text.
- **Avoid:** theatrical framing, misleading retouching, credibility-manufacturing color grades, decorative stock imagery, synthetic imagery presented as evidence

## Layout

- **Radius:** 0px by default; native control geometry may remain where it protects usability
- **Border weight:** 1px
- **Spacing:** 6px typographic unit with 6, 12, 18, 24, 36, 48, 72 and 96px steps

### Posture rules

- Use unequal column spans, shared alignment anchors and deliberate whitespace.
- Maintain source order when layouts collapse to one coherent column.
- Use flat surfaces, zero elevation, square corners and thin structural rules.
- Use logical start/end properties and verify reflow without horizontal scrolling at 320px.
- Do not force text, controls or images to a fixed baseline height when content needs to expand.
- Primary buttons use red with near-white labels, a 48px minimum height and visible hover, pressed, focus, loading and disabled states.
- Secondary buttons and inputs use near-white or pale fills with charcoal labels and 1px charcoal boundaries.
- Links remain underlined; labels stay visible; current, error and status states never rely on color alone.
- Use a 3px focus outline offset 3px: near-black on light or cyan surfaces and near-white on red or dark surfaces.
- The component kit covers buttons, text links, labeled fields, navigation, content panels, tables, tags, alerts and progress, including focus, error, loading and disabled guidance.
- No shared brand motion language is evidenced; motion is disabled by default, and product-specific transitions require a functional reason and a reduced-motion alternative.
- Generated ramps are implementation conveniences; exact verified text and action pairings remain authoritative.
