What ships in a system?

  • Colour and light tokens
  • Condensed display role
  • Flat panel grammar
How is elevation handled?

Through value shift against the near-black stage — no shadow on these panels.

Where does colour live?

  • Radial glows only
  • Violet as atmosphere
  • Ember once, at a midpoint
Can accents be fills?

No. Buttons, chips and card fills stay achromatic by design.

What carries headlines?

  • Microgramma D Extended
  • -2.7px tracking
  • Mixed-case, all-caps feel
And body copy?

Suisse carries every non-display role from 14px labels to 24px leads.
