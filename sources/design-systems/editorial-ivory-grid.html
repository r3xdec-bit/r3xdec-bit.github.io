FAQ & signup band

6 rows + form
How does the grid stay editorial?

A strict 1328px column, hairline dividers, and generous whitespace do the structural work — not shadow or colour.

Where does colour appear?

Only inside two showcase tiles, one gradient each. Everything else stays paper-white.

What is the italic serif for?

One word or letter-cluster per headline, sized identically to the sans around it.

Is the navbar ever inset?

Never. It is a full-width, sharp-cornered, sticky glass bar with blur(56px).

How many button variants exist?

Four: primary pill, nav toggle capsule, gray secondary, and bare text link.

What drives motion?

Sub-200ms ease transitions and a single continuous scroll keyframe. No overshoot.
