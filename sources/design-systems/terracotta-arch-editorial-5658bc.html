Palette

Stone, ink, and one rationed brown

Color lives inside the hero photograph. The system itself runs on off-white stone, warm near-black ink, and a single brown reserved for one control at a time.

Type scale

Instrument Sans, one italic interruption

Everything runs at medium weight with tight negative tracking. A single Lora italic clause per headline is the only serif and the only italic in the system.
