Surface & depth

Radii do the structural work

Depth comes from hairline borders — solid or inset — not drop shadows. The radius ladder spans a full pill down to a near-sharp 2px.

control999px · pills & chips
card28px · media & icon tiles
tile20px · secondary containers

Motion

Fast, with a light bounce

Transitions run in a 0.16–0.26s window. A springy overshoot handles hover and entrance; a dedicated press curve handles tile activation.
