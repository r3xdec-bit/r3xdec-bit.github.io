Body-Text Block Family ×5

The page's primary narrative rhythm — five rows at ~65% width, each a thin coral rule beside a huge headline and a paragraph.

Smooth by default

Momentum-smoothed scroll is the system's core interaction signature — inertial, weighted, never snappy.

Type on the void

Ninety-five percent flat black. Depth comes from z-layering type over a star-field, not from elevation.

Rationed coral

One decisive hue, held back until it counts — the wordmark, a keyword, a rule, a pill fill. Nothing else carries color.
