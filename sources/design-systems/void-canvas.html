Components

The confirmed inventory

Two families carry the system's entire identity: an indeterminate loader and a transform-based reveal. Each is built only from measured tokens.

Loading mechanism

An indeterminate spinner centered on an otherwise blank white viewport. Driven by rotateSpinner — a continuous, linear 360° loop. Black ink, pill radius, unobtrusive scale. The only thing allowed to paint before content is ready.

Content reveal

A one-shot, transform-driven entrance applied to a container as content enters, once loading completes. Preserves the fb_transform motion identity — a subtle translate and scale, not a decorative flourish.
