Architecture, made quiet and warm.

A gallery-minimalist editorial system. We photograph light once — terracotta archways at dusk — then let typography carry every room after it.

Palette

Stone, ink, and one brown.

Color is rationed. The canvas stays achromatic; warmth lives inside a single accent pill and the photograph itself.
