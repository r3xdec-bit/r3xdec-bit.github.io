Surface & depth

Depth from borders, not shadow

Panels are separated by hairlines and subtle value steps. Shadow is reserved for interactive glass buttons alone.

Control
radius 8px · compact buttons & inputs
Card
radius 12px · stat tiles & CTAs
Panel
radius 16px · larger panels, #111827
Chip
radius 6px · numeral & delete chips
Glass shadow
the only elevation cue — rgba(0,0,0,.1) 0 1 3, 0 1 2 -1

Functional feedback, not decoration
