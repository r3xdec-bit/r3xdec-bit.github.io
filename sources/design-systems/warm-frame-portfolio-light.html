We start narrow. One goal, one audience, one metric that actually matters to the business.

Fast, low-fidelity passes to find the shape before polishing anything into place.

03 — Build

Tokens, components, and screens assembled from the same small kit for consistency.

04 — Deliver

Handoff with everything documented, so the team can keep shipping without us.
